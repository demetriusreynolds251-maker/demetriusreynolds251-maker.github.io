How it works

📈 StockScout, step by step

☀️

Morning brief

Every stock on my watchlist gets price, growth, valuation, next earnings date and the two headlines that matter, with every finance word defined right where it's used.

📰

AI news read

Each headline is rated GOOD, BAD, MIXED or NOISE, scored 1–5 on how big a deal it is, and explained in one plain sentence.

🔎

Supplier hunter

It searches two years of 10-K filings for a big company's name and ranks the smaller suppliers and customers behind it, so lesser-known names rise to the top.

👔

Leadership check

Background from public records only: proxy statements, 8-K filings, insider-trade forms. No rumors.
